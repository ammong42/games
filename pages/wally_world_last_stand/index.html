❓ HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Defend Wally World by defeating aliens before they reach the checkout line at the bottom. If any alien passes below the grid, the game is over. How many waves can you hold them off?

Turn Order

  • Each turn, all aliens move one tile down and a new alien spawns in the top row.
  • Then it's your turn — you have 3 actions to spend.
  • When you run out of actions (or click End Turn Early), the next turn begins.
  • If the grid is cleared of aliens, your turn ends automatically.

Throwing Items

  • Click Throw Item, then click an alien to target it.
  • A die (1–6) is rolled. You hit if the roll ≥ the distance to the target (bottom row = distance 1, top row = distance 6).
  • Purchased actions (Heavy, Streamlined, Splash) can each be used once per turn.

Alien Types

  • 👽 Basic — Standard HP, no special traits.
  • 🤖 Teleporter — Spawns in row 2, slightly lower HP.
  • 🏃 Fast — Lower HP, but −1 to your accuracy roll when targeted.
  • 🐛 Horde — Fills all open lanes with weak bug aliens.
  • 👾 Boss — Every 10th alien. Double HP. Defeating it opens the shop and increases base alien HP for future waves.

Shop & Credits

Defeat a boss to open the upgrade shop. Credits earned = 10 × bosses defeated. Spend them on new actions or stat upgrades that persist for the rest of the run.
